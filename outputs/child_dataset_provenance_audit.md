# SafeKid AI — Dataset Provenance & Integrity Audit
## Comprehensive Forensic Analysis of Child Detection Training & Evaluation Data

**Project:** SafeKid AI — AI-Based Child Safety Monitoring for Gated Residential Communities  
**Audit Target:** `data/child_dataset/fixtures/`, `models/child_classifier_v1.pt`, `scripts/generate_child_dataset_fixture.py`, `scripts/train_child_detector.py`  
**Audit Date:** November 5, 2026  
**Auditor:** Automated Engineering Validation & Integrity Suite  

---

### Executive Summary

An audit of the Prompt 21 child detection subsystem was conducted to evaluate dataset provenance, split independence, and generalization validity. 

**Primary Finding:** The current child detector (`models/child_classifier_v1.pt`) was trained and evaluated **exclusively on programmatically synthesized 2D geometric fixture images**, not genuine surveillance footage. The reported 96.00% Child F1 score on the 32-sample held-out test split represents **in-distribution fixture verification**, not real-world CCTV generalization. 

Perceptual hashing (dHash) identified **393 near-duplicate pairs** across the 200 fixture samples, including **128 cross-split template collisions** between training, validation, and test sets. While the evaluation pipeline ran genuine PyTorch inference and was not hardcoded, the splits were not structurally independent.

---

### Detailed Findings on the 9 Audit Questions

#### 1. Image Sourcing & Synthetic vs. Real Origin
- **Finding:** **100% of the fixture images are purely synthetic geometric renderings.**
- **Evidence:** All 200 images in `data/child_dataset/fixtures/images/` were generated by `scripts/generate_child_dataset_fixture.py` via `render_pedestrian_crop()`. The function renders circles for heads, rectangles for torsos, rectangles for legs, and ellipses for hair on a uniform gray canvas ($120 \pm 15$ tone with linear gradient).
- **Surveillance Realism:** Zero images were extracted from actual CCTV streams, residential security cameras, or public surveillance archives. Real-world physical characteristics—complex clothing textures, loose fabric, footwear, natural walking gait dynamics, non-upright postures (bending, cycling, running), optical lens distortion, and outdoor background clutter—are completely absent.

#### 2. Label Verification & Annotation Methodology
- **Finding:** **Labels are synthetic parameters assigned by code, not independently adjudicated ground truth.**
- **Evidence:** Labels (`CHILD`, `ADULT`, `AMBIGUOUS`) were directly inherited from loop logic in the generator (`is_child=True` $\to$ `ground_truth="CHILD"`). No human annotator, inter-rater reliability protocol, or independent validation verified the visual plausibility of the labels.

#### 3. Visual Diversity & Demographic Realism
- **Finding:** **Visual diversity is minimal and structurally constrained.**
- **Evidence:** The generator iterates over fixed finite lists:
  - 9 clothing color triplets: Red `(220, 60, 40)`, Gold `(40, 180, 220)`, Orange `(50, 140, 220)`, Blue `(200, 120, 40)`, Green `(80, 190, 80)`, Purple `(160, 80, 180)`, Charcoal `(45, 45, 50)`, Grey `(180, 180, 180)`, Navy `(50, 80, 140)`.
  - 5 skin tone triplets.
  - Modulo indexing (`i % len(clothing_palettes)`) cycles the same color schemes repeatedly.
- **Consequence:** Visual variation consists only of randomized bounding dimensions ($\pm 20$ pixels) applied to the identical geometric archetype.

#### 4. Duplicate & Near-Duplicate Analysis
- **Finding:** **Extensive near-duplicate redundancy exists across the dataset.**
- **Evidence:** An automated difference hash (dHash, 64-bit) audit conducted across all 200 samples revealed:
  - **393 pairwise near-duplicate collisions** with Hamming distance $\le 2$.
  - Distinct filenames share near-identical pixel arrangements. For example, `crop_child_0001.png` and `crop_child_0010.png` share the same red clothing, identical torso geometry, and identical background, differing only by 5 pixels in canvas height.

#### 5. Cross-Split Independence & Template Leakage
- **Finding:** **Genuinely independent splits were NOT achieved.**
- **Evidence:** Of the 393 near-duplicate pairs, **128 pairs crossed split boundaries** (Train $\leftrightarrow$ Val, Train $\leftrightarrow$ Test, Val $\leftrightarrow$ Test).
- **Root Cause:** Samples were partitioned by index range (`i < 56` $\to$ train, `56 <= i < 68` $\to$ val, `i >= 68` $\to$ test) without grouping by subject archetype. Because the generator cycled through the same 9 clothing styles, identical visual archetypes were deposited into both the training and test splits.
- **Conclusion:** The model was tested on structural templates it had already memorized during training.

#### 6. Data Augmentation Isolation
- **Finding:** **Data augmentation did not leak transformed image bytes, but did not mitigate template leakage.**
- **Evidence:** In `scripts/train_child_detector.py`, horizontal flipping, color jitter, and random cutout were dynamically applied inside `PedestrianCropDataset.__getitem__` with `augment=True` for the training split only. The validation and test sets used raw unaugmented crops. However, because the underlying base images were near-duplicates, augmentation did not prevent template overfitting.

#### 7. Model Checkpoint & Weight Provenance
- **Finding:** **Evaluation correctly evaluated the actual trained checkpoint.**
- **Evidence:** `scripts/evaluate_child_detector.py` directly loaded `models/child_classifier_v1.pt`, which was serialized by `train_child_detector.py` via `torch.save()`. SHA-256 integrity and checkpoint metadata were confirmed.

#### 8. Inclusion of Ambiguous & UNKNOWN Samples
- **Finding:** **UNKNOWN and ambiguous samples were appropriately evaluated.**
- **Evidence:** The held-out test split included 8 AMBIGUOUS samples (distant figures $< 36$px, severe occlusions $> 50\%$, and borderline teens). The evaluation recorded 6 abstentions to `UNKNOWN` (75.0% Ambiguous Abstention Rate) and populated a complete $3 \times 3$ confusion matrix.

#### 9. Calculation of Reported Metrics
- **Finding:** **Reported metrics are genuine empirical calculations, not hardcoded simulations.**
- **Evidence:** All TP, FP, FN, precision, recall, and latency values were computed by executing `detector.classify_crop()` on disk images during test execution. No metrics were manufactured or hardcoded.

### Audit Conclusions & Recommendations

1. **Honest Performance Representation:** The 96.00% Child F1 metric must be clearly designated as **Synthetic Fixture In-Distribution Verification**, not real-world performance.
2. **Preventing Shortcut Learning:** The model must not rely solely on flat gray backgrounds or unnormalized crop dimensions. Camera perspective calibration, ground-contact point scale, and explicit body proportion ratios must be integrated.
3. **Real-World Milestone Status:** Real-world training on genuine surveillance datasets remains **BLOCKED** until external academic datasets (RAP v2) are acquired under signed institutional agreements.
